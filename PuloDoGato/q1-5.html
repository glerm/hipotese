📐 Q1-5: Expressão Numérica

Enunciado:

\[ \frac{1}{5} + \left\{ \left[ \frac{4}{9} + \left( \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{4} - \frac{1}{9} \right) \right] \right\} \]
Passo 1 – Multiplicar dentro dos parênteses: \[ \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{2}{4} = \frac{2}{8} = \frac{1}{4} \]
Passo 2 – Subtrair \(\frac{1}{9}\): \[ \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{9}{36} - \frac{4}{36} = \frac{5}{36} \]
Passo 3 – Somar com \(\frac{4}{9}\): \[ \frac{4}{9} + \frac{5}{36} = \frac{16}{36} + \frac{5}{36} = \frac{21}{36} = \frac{7}{12} \]
Passo 4 – Somar com \(\frac{1}{5}\): \[ \frac{1}{5} + \frac{7}{12} = \frac{12}{60} + \frac{35}{60} = \frac{47}{60} \]

✅ Resposta:

\[ \boxed{\frac{47}{60}} \]
